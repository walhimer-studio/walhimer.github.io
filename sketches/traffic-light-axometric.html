I was walking home when I looked up at the stoplight and thought there was a camera mounted on top of it. Then the camera moved, and I realized it was a bird nesting on the light.

Three.js sketch

Machine in the city, and a small living thing. The traffic light keeps doing its work. The bird has found a warm place above it, its back to the mechanics, at ease. What looked like a camera from a distance, on top of a technical device, was, all along, a living thing. The bird faces away from the traffic—away from the red, yellow, green—turned toward the quiet side, the sky, indifferent to the signal’s job.

I have become so accustomed to surveillance cameras and security devices that when it is a living thing, I am surprised, amused, and relieved.
