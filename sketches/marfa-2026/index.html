Invisible Layer

One system — the invisible layer of the screen — as a free signed card, a living screen piece, and an optional digital twin.

I started coding in 2022 while my mother was dying. It became a refuge — total focus, like painting. After she died, I understood what had been running underneath all along.

One hundred cards, hand-numbered 1/100 to 100/100. Tap the NFC on the back to open your piece. Cards are free. The digital edition is available on Transient Labs if you want to keep it.
