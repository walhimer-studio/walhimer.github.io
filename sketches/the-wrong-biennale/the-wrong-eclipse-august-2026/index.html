The Wrong Eclipse

Open call for The Wrong Eclipse, 12 August 2026 · exhibition 24 hours · submission deadline 1 August 2026. Geographic anchor: The Wrong Biennale, Partida Maitino 2069c, Alicante. Listed on the main catalog.

Listening to the Sky — code that listens to the partial eclipse through the atmosphere above Maitino. The sky is the exhibition space; the piece is its soundtrack. One Machine DNA lifeline drives sonification and the Bloom garden (life bar, obscuration) from the same state — not audio-reactive visuals.

Pentatonic Salamander piano, binaural L/R field, live atmospheric inputs (weather, traffic, orbit). Bandcamp holds a proof of concept; the authoritative work is the live re-recording during the eclipse on 12 August 2026 (~19:40–21:26 CEST, max obscuration ~99.1%).
