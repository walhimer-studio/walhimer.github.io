Miradas Tangentes

Two paired organisms take turns on one vertical screen — warm and cool color families in saturated translucent layers. Each organism runs three minutes with a seamless crossfade loop, formatted for exhibition display (1080×1920).

Surrender Machine — Madrid

Time-based lifecycle edition for the Madrid show: white void, platform emergence, columns, pulleys, gears, rods, full running state, then fade to white — surrender. Three-minute loop aligned to exhibition timing.
