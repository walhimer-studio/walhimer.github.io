## 1. What the Collector receives
The Work is a generative artwork that runs **live, in real time**, on a dedicated computer and monitor supplied by the Artist (the "Unit"). It is generated on the Unit, not played back from a recording. The Unit boots directly to the Work, runs entirely on the machine, and needs no internet connection. A certificate with the Work's title, edition number, seed, unit ID, and file checksum comes with the Unit. The Unit holds only the Work the Collector bought.
[If sold as a bundle: the Collector also receives the plotter drawing and digital file numbered ______, which carry the same seed.]

## 2. Ownership and license
The Collector owns the physical Unit. The Artist keeps the copyright in the Work and its code. The Collector has a license to display the Work on the Unit, privately or publicly, as an artwork. The Collector may not copy, modify, redistribute, or run the Work on any other device, or sell reproductions.

## 3. Offline operation and frozen software
The Unit is delivered with networking off, and the Work never needs a connection. The software on the Unit is fixed and has automatic updates turned off, so the Work looks the same over time. The Collector may connect the Unit to the internet only for optional remote review (Section 4). The Artist will not change or replace the Work on a sold Unit over the network.

## 4. Optional remote review
If the Collector chooses to connect the Unit, the Artist may check its status (uptime, temperature, which piece is running, and a screenshot of what is on screen). Nothing else is accessed. The Collector may disconnect at any time.

## 5. Display and care
The monitor supplied with the Unit is [model ____] and its settings are locked. The Collector will not change its brightness or color settings. The Collector will keep the Unit powered, ventilated, and in a normal indoor environment. The Collector will not open the Unit, install software, or remove or change the files on it. The Unit is set to restart on its own after a power outage.

## 6. Replacement every [2–3] years
The Unit has a limited life. Every [two to three] years, the Artist will send the Collector a new, identical Unit loaded with the same Work, with a prepaid return label. The Collector will swap it in and send the old Unit back within [30] days. The Artist will [pay for / split] the replacement hardware and shipping: __________.

## 7. Return and destruction
The Artist will wipe and physically destroy every returned Unit, and will give the Collector a record showing the unit ID, date, and method. The Collector may not keep the old Unit or its files.

## 8. Repairs
If the Unit fails between replacements, the Collector will contact the Artist. The Artist will repair or replace it [at no charge during __ years / at the Collector's cost for damage the Collector caused].

## 9. Resale and transfer
The Collector may resell or give the Unit to another person only with the Artist's written consent, and only if the new owner signs this agreement. The replacement schedule continues with the new owner.

## 10. Authenticity and records
The Artist will keep the source files, the seed, and a record of the Work's checksum, so the Unit can be verified as genuine and replaced if needed. If the Artist can no longer do so, the Artist will arrange for [named successor/estate] to keep these records.

## 12. Referral fee (separate from the Collector)
If a third party introduces the sale, the Artist will pay that person 5% of the net sale price under a separate written agreement with them. This does not change the Collector's price or rights.
