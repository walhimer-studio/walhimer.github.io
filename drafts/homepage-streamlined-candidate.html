Blockchains, wallets, and whether you are about to lose your money

Am I going to lose all my money?

The single most common thing said to me, usually in exactly those words. It deserves a direct, honest answer rather than reassurance — including the parts that genuinely are risky and the parts that are not.

to write — lead with this one

What is a blockchain, in one paragraph?

Without metaphors about ledgers and villages. What it actually does, why anyone bothered, and what it means for an artwork.

to write

What are the major blockchains, and why do artists use different ones?

Ethereum, Tezos, and the others — what distinguishes them in practice, including cost and energy, which is the objection that comes up second.

How do I set up a wallet?

Step by step, with screenshots, assuming no prior knowledge. What a seed phrase is, why it matters, and the specific mistakes that cause people to actually lose things.

Partly yes. Which parts, why, and what to do about each. Saying so plainly is what makes the rest of the page believable.
