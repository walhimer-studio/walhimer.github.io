Where it's going

Everything on the previous two pages is a matter of record. This page is not. These are my predictions, and I am stating them plainly so you can hold them against what actually happens.

Why a page of predictions rather than a survey: a survey tells you what exists. A prediction tells you what to plan for, and it can be wrong in public. If you are deciding whether to commit budget to something over a ten-year horizon, the second is more useful.
Draft note — this page is yours to write Below are the slots and the framing. The predictions themselves have to be yours: they are the whole value of the page, and they are the thing that positions you as someone with a view rather than someone summarizing other people's. Aim for five or six, each stated so specifically that it could turn out to be wrong.
