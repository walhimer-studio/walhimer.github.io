Immersive digital art — what you saw, and how to talk about bringing it home

You saw a room or a wall of moving image in a museum, a lobby, or a public space. The words that get you started are immersive digital art and digital art installation. This series teaches the sharper vocabulary next, then the practical questions — cost, public art, small museum — that decide whether anything happens.

I am an artist who makes this work. Opinions are marked as such. The terms, the institutional history, and the maintenance questions are not.
