16-page PDF, 396 × 612 pt. Issue 01 opens the first layer: the code itself, and Art Blocks Marfa.

## Statement

Invisible Layers is a monthly magazine of work made with code, real-time systems and artificial intelligence. The name is also the subject. On the surface the pieces are technical: seeds, frame rates, render loops, source code. Underneath they are about things that are hard to say out loud — accepting what can't be controlled, finding where agency begins and ends, asking what remains of a person inside the systems we build.

The method has a lineage: the Situationists' détournement, Gilbert Simondon's open technical objects, hacker culture's habit of opening things up. The question is whether computation can carry an inner life.
