# Witness Mirror (1s)

Technology as witness, a tool that registers affect you may not be admitting, or renders what was invisible, or asks you to surrender to a process you don't fully control.

Same as [Witness Mirror](./witness-mirror.md) — testimony + machine witness — with **1-second** DeepFace samples instead of 5s. Tighter sync (~±0.5s) for moment-by-moment audit: grief in the story, fear on the face, masking.
