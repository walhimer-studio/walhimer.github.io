# Face signal thought replay

Face crossfade plus **mind layer** — each mood loads stance + passage from `thought-map.json` and `sessions/thoughts/*.json`.

**Live:** https://mark-walhimer.com/sketches/digital-twin/face-signal-thought-replay.html

Press **1–7** to crossfade portrait PNGs and load testimony from `thought-map.json` / `sessions/thoughts/*.json`. Moods follow **DeepFace** (angry, disgust, fear, happy, sad, surprise, neutral). Keys 1–3 use nearest face PNG until dedicated stills exist.
