## Concept (curators)

The eclipse itself is the visual artwork. The exhibition invites listeners to **hear** while experiencing the real eclipse outdoors — less screen time, more sky. Sound only; no video or screen-dependent entries.

## Geographic anchor

The Wrong Biennale — **Partida Maitino 2069c, Alicante, 03295, Spain**. This site is the atmospheric and astronomical clock for works in this folder.

### Eclipse at Maitino / Alicante · 12 August 2026

Partial eclipse only — **no totality**. Maximum obscuration **~99.1%** (~0.9% of the solar disc still visible).

| Event | Time (CEST) |
|-------|-------------|
| First contact | ~19:39:59 |
| Maximum eclipse | ~20:34:59 |
| Above ~99% obscuration | ~20:34 – 20:36 (~2 minutes) |
| Last contact | ~21:25:59 |
| Full partial arc | ~1h 46m |

Sources: [Time.now — Alicante](https://time.now/alicante/eclipses/2026-08-12/) (Maitino is in Alicante province; times match Alicante city within seconds).
